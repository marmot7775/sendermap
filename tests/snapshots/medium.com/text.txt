What to fix
  Problems found in the setup, most serious first.

  1 ▲ Seven DKIM keys at medium.com are 1024-bit: google (Google Workspace), s2 (SendGrid), smtpapi 
      (SendGrid), intercom (Intercom), cka (Kit), mandrill (Mandrill) and mx                        
      Some receivers already treat 1024-bit RSA keys as weak. Rotate to 2048-bit.                   
  2 ▲ The SendGrid key smtpapi at email.medium.com is 1024-bit                   
      Some receivers already treat 1024-bit RSA keys as weak. Rotate to 2048-bit.
  3 ▲ Google Workspace at mail.medium.com has no SPF
